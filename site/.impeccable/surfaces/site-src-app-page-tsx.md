Scope: Saffarr Travels home page (one-page scroll) plus blog routes. Visitor mode: Persuade (home), Read (blog posts).
Audience: Pune corporate travellers and airport-run customers; secondary weekend travellers. Action: call or WhatsApp to book.
Proof on hand: customers praise driving skill and talkative drivers; "treated like family" story. No numbers, quotes or prices may be invented.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Saffarr is told in the language of the Indian highway: green guide signs, white kilometre stones with yellow caps, lane paint. Distance and time are the content. Refuses the category default of a stock car-on-highway hero with price banners and icon feature cards.
OWN-WORLD: Sign green field (committed, a third or more of screens), retroreflective white, asphalt charcoal, milestone-cap yellow for the one action. One family, Overpass (Highway Gothic lineage), tabular figures for km and times. Sign panels with thick inset white border and rounded corners; milestones as white stone shapes with yellow caps; lane-paint dashes as the only linework.
STORY: First you see a 4:30 a.m. pickup sign: "On time. On first-name terms." You learn it began with one car and grew by repeat requests, meet the drivers, pick a car by occasion, and plan airport or weekend trips by distance and leave-by time. You call or WhatsApp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed night photo of Pune Airport. A large green guide sign panel, left of centre, spanning about 60% width: "04:30 Pickup" line, H1 "On time. On first-name terms.", one sentence, yellow "Book your ride" button and a call link; customer-photo row beside the button. Right edge: a milestone stone reading PUNE AIRPORT 14 km.
