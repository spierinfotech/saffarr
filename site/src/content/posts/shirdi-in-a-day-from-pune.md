**Short answer:** Shirdi is about **185 km from Pune, roughly 4.5 hours by road**. Leave at 5 a.m., and you can complete darshan and be back in Pune by 7 or 8 p.m. **Weekdays other than Thursday** usually have the shortest queues.

## The day, hour by hour

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 5:00 a.m. | Pickup from home |
| 7:00 a.m. | Breakfast stop on the way |
| 9:30 a.m. | Reach Shirdi |
| 9:45 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. | Darshan (2 to 4 hours depending on the crowd) |
| 1:00 p.m. | Lunch |
| 2:00 p.m. | Start back to Pune |
| 7:00 p.m. | Home |

## Making darshan easier

- **Go on a weekday.** Thursdays, weekends and festival days are the busiest.
- **Check the official booking options** for darshan passes before you go, since rules and slots change.
- **Leave valuables in the car** with your driver, and carry only what you need into the temple.

## Adding Shani Shingnapur

Shani Shingnapur is about 70 km from Shirdi. Adding it makes for a long day of roughly 15 hours. If you have elderly travellers with you, consider an overnight stay in Shirdi instead.
