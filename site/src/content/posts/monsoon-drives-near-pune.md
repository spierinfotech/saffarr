**Short answer:** the best monsoon drives from Pune are **Lonavala and Khandala, Tamhini Ghat, Mulshi Lake, Bhimashankar and Malshej Ghat**. All five work as day trips. Go on a weekday if you can, and leave by 6 a.m.

## 1. Lonavala and Khandala

About 66 km and 1.5 hours from Pune. Bhushi Dam overflows, Tiger's Point disappears into cloud, and the old highway through Khandala Ghat is lined with waterfalls. Stop for chikki in Lonavala town on the way back.

## 2. Tamhini Ghat

About 50 km from Pune via Mulshi. Narrow, green and full of small waterfalls right beside the road. Our drivers like it because it's quieter than Lonavala, even on Sundays.

## 3. Mulshi Lake

Combine it with Tamhini. The lake fills up through July, and the roads around it are some of the prettiest near the city.

## 4. Bhimashankar

About 110 km and 3 hours. The Jyotirlinga temple sits in a wildlife sanctuary that's almost always in cloud during the monsoon. Wear shoes with grip; the steps get slippery.

## 5. Malshej Ghat

About 125 km and 3 hours. Flamingos in some years, waterfalls in every year, and a long ghat road that's the reason to go.

## Before you go

- **Leave early.** Weekend traffic on the monsoon routes builds after 9 a.m.
- **Carry a change of clothes.** You will get wet, whether or not you plan to.
- **Respect the warnings.** Some waterfall points close after heavy rain. Our drivers will tell you which ones are safe on the day.
- **Book an experienced driver.** Ghat roads in fog and rain are where driving skill matters most.
