**Short answer:** for a domestic flight from Mumbai, leave Pune about **6 to 7 hours before departure**. For an international flight, leave **7 to 8 hours before**. That covers roughly 3.5 to 4 hours on the road, a 45-minute buffer, and the time your airline wants you at the terminal.

## The three numbers that decide your pickup time

1. **Drive time.** From west Pune (Hinjewadi, Wakad, Baner) the Expressway is close, so plan on about 3 hours 15 minutes. From east Pune (Kharadi, Viman Nagar, Hadapsar) add another 30 to 45 minutes to cross the city first.
2. **Buffer.** We add about 45 minutes for the ghat section, the Expressway tolls and Mumbai's last few kilometres, which are the least predictable part of the trip.
3. **Airport time.** Most airlines ask you to arrive 2 hours before domestic flights and 3 hours before international ones.

Add them up and count backwards from your departure time. Or use the [pickup planner](/#planner) on our home page, which does the arithmetic for your area.

## A worked example

Your flight leaves Mumbai at 9:00 a.m. and you live in Kharadi.

- Airport arrival: 7:00 a.m. (2 hours before a domestic flight)
- Buffer: 45 minutes, so 6:15 a.m.
- Drive from Kharadi: about 4 hours, so **pickup at 2:15 a.m.**

That sounds early. It is. But night drives are the calmest of the week, and most people sleep through the ghat.

## What changes the answer

- **Weekday mornings into Mumbai** are slower after about 7 a.m. An early pickup avoids that entirely.
- **Monsoon (June to September)** brings fog and slow traffic in the ghat. Add 30 minutes.
- **Long weekends** fill the Expressway in both directions. Leave earlier than you think.
