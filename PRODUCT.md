## Platform

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) with static export, Tailwind CSS, GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Lenis smooth scroll, MDX blog from local files. Hosted on Vercel. Canonical domain: https://saffarr.com (user-confirmed).

## Users

- **Corporate travellers in Pune** (primary): employees and managers working in business districts such as Hinjewadi, Kharadi, Magarpatta, Baner and Viman Nagar who need punctual cars for meetings, client visits, offsites and office runs, often booked by themselves or an admin.
- **Airport-run customers** (primary): people flying out of Pune Airport (Lohegaon) or Mumbai Airport, frequently at odd hours (pre-dawn), whose job is "get me there on time without stress".
- **Weekend and outstation travellers** (secondary): families and groups taking trips from Pune across Maharashtra.

## Product Purpose

Saffarr Travels rents cars with experienced drivers from Pune for airport transfers, corporate travel and outstation trips across Maharashtra. The website must turn a visitor into a call or WhatsApp booking, and give returning and AI-assisted searchers clear, citable answers about the service. It also hosts a blog ("Driver's Notes") with route and destination guidance.

## Positioning

The business started with one car and grew because customers kept asking for the same driver again. Ride apps give anonymous drivers, cancellations and surge pricing; Saffarr gives a familiar, skilled driver who is on time. Tagline: "On time. On first-name terms."

## Product Principles

1. The driver is the product; the car is the vessel.
2. Reliability first, warmth second, and never warmth at the cost of reliability.
3. One tap to a human: phone and WhatsApp always within reach.
4. Say only what is true; placeholders over invented proof.
5. Answer real questions plainly so people and AI assistants can quote us.
